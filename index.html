Rules and sources

  • Canadian fixed-rate mortgage interest is quoted with semi-annual compounding (Interest Act, s.6): Justice Laws
  • Minimum qualifying rate for uninsured mortgages: the greater of the contract rate plus 2% or 5.25%: OSFI
  • GDS up to 39% and TDS up to 44% for insured mortgages: CMHC
  • Prepayment penalty on fixed-rate mortgages is usually the higher of three months' interest or the interest rate differential: FCAC
  • US comparison uses a 30-year fixed with monthly compounding. US figures are for comparison only.
